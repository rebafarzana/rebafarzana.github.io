# rebafarzana.github.io
Personal website of Reba Farzana

I am an Earth observation and environmental researcher with a background in Disaster Science and Management, Geoinformation Science, and Natural Resource Management. My research interests lie at the intersection of remote sensing, climate extremes, urban heat islands, ecosystem resilience, and environmental modelling. I am particularly interested in using Earth observation and geospatial data to understand environmental change and climate-related risks.
